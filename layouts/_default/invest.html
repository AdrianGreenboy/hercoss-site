Questions

Why can't I pay right now?

Because we want your investment to be properly protected. Hercoss is being incorporated, and an offer of company shares to the public is regulated in Germany and in Mexico. As soon as the legal structure is in place, everyone who registered receives the formal documents first.

Who can see how much I pledged?

Only you and Hercoss. Other investors see the pool total and their own share, never names or e-mail addresses.

Can I change or withdraw my pledge?

Yes, at any time in the Investor Hub, and you can delete all your data with one button.

Euros or pesos?

Both. You pledge in your currency; the pool shows the total in euros, converted at the European Central Bank reference rate, and in each currency separately.
